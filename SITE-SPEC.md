## Design system
- Premium dark AI/SaaS theme: deep navy backgrounds, purple → cyan → blue gradients, glassmorphism, glows, floating glass navbar.
- Cinematic landing entrance: staggered reveals (navbar → headline → subtext → CTA → visual), animated gradient text, ambient background effects, reduced-motion support.
- Product grid: **4 products per row** (desktop), category-by-category grouping, sorted lowest→highest price within each category. Compact ~500px cards, 26px gutters, equal-height rows.
- Product imagery: full-bleed 16:10 frames, strictly clipped to card bounds (no overflow), consistent across cards and detail views.
- Price display: bold tilted **red "PRICE" tag** with large discounted numerals + overlapping **yellow "Regular Price" sticker** with struck-through regular price.
- Warranty display: teal pill badge with checkmark icon, labeled "**N Days/Months Replacement Warranty**" — identical style on every product, cards and detail views.
- Buttons: professional hover effects — smooth color transitions + subtle lift/glow, consistent everywhere.
- Footer: compact centered premium panel — brand + tagline, Quick Links, top categories, contact (WhatsApp with official logo, Community, Channel, Facebook), bottom bar "© 2026 AI Tools HuB. All rights reserved."

## Page sections / features
1. Hero + trust strip (Clear plan terms / Replacement coverage / WhatsApp support — symmetric spacing).
2. Product catalog with working category filters.
3. Product detail views: name, price, duration, credits/features, Replacement Warranty, activation/login process, claim guidance, WhatsApp support, add-to-cart.
4. Cart + 3-step checkout ("Choose your plan → Pay by Easypaisa or Binance → Confirm on WhatsApp"), compact payment card with copy buttons.
5. FAQ, warranty guidance, community access (Community + Channel + Facebook).
6. **AI Tools HuB Assistant** (floating chat): trilingual (English / Urdu / Roman Urdu) with language selector + auto-detection; product knowledge base; conversational small talk; product-availability intelligence ("gpt hai?" → not-available + alternatives); "tell me about X" → real product descriptions; unknown queries → wait message + one-tap WhatsApp handoff with pre-filled question to 03493634347.
7. Site-wide language toggle: English by default, one-tap **Roman Urdu** translation of static content.
